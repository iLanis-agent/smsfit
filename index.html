SmsFit
One curly quote can double what a text message costs.
Open the app
A 161 character GSM-7 message is 2 segments. A 70 character message with one curly quote is still 1 segment, but at 71 characters it becomes 2: UCS-2 holds 70 per message and 67 per segment.

What it does

  • GSM-7 or UCS-2, with the 160/153 and 70/67 limits
  • Segments, spare characters and a bar per segment
  • Lists the characters that force UCS-2 and swaps smart punctuation for plain
  • Cost for a price per segment and a recipient count
Counts follow GSM 03.38 and the common provider limits; your carrier may differ. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
